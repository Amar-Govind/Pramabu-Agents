# Parambu Organics — Grow Kit Seed Packets

Front artwork for the seed pockets that ship inside the Parambu Organics Organic Farming Kit.

Every packet uses the same template so the set reads as one range:

- Kraft-beige recycled paper background with a deep-green double-line border and rose-gold corner flourishes
- Parambu Organics palm-leaf mark and wordmark locked to the top
- Botanical illustration of the plant with its fruit, flower and leaves
- Crop name in English (plus variety line where relevant) and in Tamil
- "Organic Seeds · Open Pollinated (Not Hybrid)" caption
- Sowing window, germination time and sunlight icons, then net quantity and `parambu.in`

Palette follows `brand/brand_bible.yaml`: kraft beige `#F1E4CE`, deep forest green `#1B4332`, champagne rose-gold `#D6AA84`.

All files are 1024 × 1536 px PNG (3:4 portrait), which is roughly 370 DPI on a 7 cm wide packet.
